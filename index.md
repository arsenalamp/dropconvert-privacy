# DropConvert — Privacy Policy & Support

*Last updated: November 2026*

## Privacy Policy

DropConvert is designed with your privacy as a top priority.

- **100% On-Device Image Conversion:** All image conversions and processing happen entirely locally on your device. Your images never leave your device and are never uploaded to external servers.
- **No Data Collection:** DropConvert does not collect, store, track, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or file contents.
- **In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2):** Optional Pro upgrades are processed securely by Apple through StoreKit 2. DropConvert does not receive or store your payment information. For details on how Apple handles purchase data, please refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
